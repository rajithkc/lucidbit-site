FAQ

Does it cancel subscriptions for me?

No. Subscription Calendar tracks your plans and reminds you of important dates. You still cancel with the service provider or through the App Store. Recording a cancellation in the app does not cancel the service itself.

Can I track subscriptions in different currencies?

Yes. Each subscription keeps its billing currency, while summaries and equivalent amounts use your chosen main currency.

How does importing work?

Choose App Store subscription screenshots or a supported file. The app prepares a review and flags details that need checking. Essential includes one completed import; Pro unlocks unlimited imports.

Does it work on both iPhone and Mac?

Yes. The app supports iOS 18 and later and macOS 15 and later. Each platform is sold separately. Pro includes optional iCloud sync, while widgets are available on both.
