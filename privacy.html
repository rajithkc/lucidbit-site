Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

LucidBit builds apps for the Apple ecosystem. This policy explains how information is handled by our apps, including Subscription Calendar, and by our website.

The short version

We do not operate a user-account service or add advertising trackers to Subscription Calendar. Your subscription records are stored on your device. Optional iCloud sync and online features communicate with the services described below.

Subscription Calendar

The app stores the subscription details you enter, including service names, amounts, currencies, dates, lists, categories, payment-method labels and notes. Payment-method labels are for your own records; the app does not require card numbers or bank credentials. Preferences, cached service logos and widget snapshots are also stored locally.

Screenshot recognition and import parsing take place on your device. Imported screenshots and files are not uploaded to LucidBit for recognition. Notifications are scheduled locally through Apple's notification system.

Optional iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your subscription records are stored in Apple's CloudKit service associated with your Apple account and synchronised across your devices. LucidBit does not receive those records through a separate developer server. Apple's terms and privacy policy apply to iCloud. Turning sync off stops future syncing; it does not by itself delete records already stored in iCloud.

Online service lookup and logos

When you search for a service that needs an online lookup, its name or search text may be sent to Apple's App Store search service or Brandfetch. To retrieve a logo, a service domain may be sent to logo.dev, Google, DuckDuckGo or the service's own website. These requests reveal your IP address and standard request information to the receiving provider. The app does not send subscription amounts, notes or payment-method labels with these requests. Providers handle requests under their own privacy policies.

Currency conversion

Subscription Calendar requests exchange rates from Frankfurter using currency codes. Subscription amounts and names are not included in the rate request. Converted amounts are calculated on your device. The rate provider receives your IP address and standard request information.

Analytics and tracking

Subscription Calendar does not include a third-party advertising or analytics SDK, and it does not send app-usage analytics to LucidBit. Apple may process diagnostics and App Store information according to your device settings and Apple's policies.

Local storage and deletion

LucidBit apps store preferences and the data needed for their features on your device. In Subscription Calendar, you can delete records or use the app's reset controls. Deleting synced records may also remove them from your other devices. Removing an app does not necessarily remove its iCloud data or all local data on macOS.

Website and Newsletter

Our website offers an optional email newsletter. If you choose to subscribe, we collect your email address solely for the purpose of sending you infrequent updates about new releases.

We use ConvertKit (Kit) as our email service provider. By subscribing, you acknowledge that your email address will be transferred to ConvertKit for processing in accordance with their privacy policy. We will never sell, rent, or spam your email address, and you can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any email we send.

App Store purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store and Mac App Store. Subscription Calendar uses StoreKit transaction information to verify purchases and restore access. LucidBit does not receive or store your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs that transaction.

Changes to this policy

If we ever update this policy in a meaningful way, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date. Please review the updated policy when new features are introduced.
