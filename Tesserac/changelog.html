1.0 May 2026 Latest

Initial release. A spatial app switcher for macOS that lives quietly in the menu bar.

  • Multiple layouts: Spatial Tile, Glass Orb, Grid, and List — switch between immersive and compact workflows instantly.
  • Keyboard-driven navigation: Hold a modifier key or use a custom shortcut to summon Tesserac anywhere.
  • Fuzzy filtering: Type to instantly filter and jump between running apps.
  • Invoke methods: Supports hold keys, custom shortcuts, middle mouse button, and Tab dwell launch.
  • Favourites: Pin important apps so they always appear in the switcher.
  • Ignored Apps: Permanently hide apps you never want to see.
  • Appearance: Configure layouts, animations, and behaviour with live previews.
  • Menu-bar home: No Dock icon, no clutter. Tesserac is always ready when you need it.
  • Privacy: No analytics, no tracking, no telemetry. Everything stays local to your Mac.
